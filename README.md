# CS330
Repository for SNHU Class CS-330 (Comp Graphic and Visualization)

When it comes to designing software, I often find myself getting overwhelmed at the idea of tackling all of the requirements of a task at once.
Because of this, it's often helpful for my sanity to break the requirements down into modularized pieces, accomplishing one part of the 
problem at a time. This was very much so the case of how my workflow went throughout this course, taking the project step by step, until all needs were met.
This started with building the meshes for the shapes in the final scence, then building on camera functionality, texturing, and finally lighting. 
In a nutshell, this just encapsulates the idea of iteration, by continually improving on a project, bit by bit, until it reaches a more improved form
(rather than waiting until a massive amount of work has been completed before assessing if it aligns with the project goals).
I had a very good time with this course, as it allowed me to work on technologies that I haven't really had a chance to try before this point, and let me express
a level of creativity that wasn't as present in previous courses. I'm excited to see how my creativity may be able to inform my work in the rest of my courses
in the future.
